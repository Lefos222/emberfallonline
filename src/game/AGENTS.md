- Farm plots are drawn over the painted field in `src/assets/farm-scene.png`; `PLOT_SPOTS` in `src/game/Farm.tsx` holds each plot centre as a % of the picture, so replacing the art means re-measuring them. Seed kinds and their gold/Farm XP payouts all live in `src/game/farm.ts` (`SEED_KINDS`, `seedGold`, `seedXp`).
- Mounts (`src/game/mounts.ts`), runes (`runes.ts`), the VIP Black Market (`black-market.ts`) and the Wheel of Fortune share one panel module, `src/game/Emporium.tsx`. Gold mounts live in `hero.mounts`, Diamond mounts in server-owned `srv.mounts` via `premium_service('mount_<id>')` whose SQL price table mirrors `MOUNTS`; the wheel prize is rolled only by the `wheel_spin` RPC; rune ranks join Renown's slot in `computeStats`. Why: Diamond spending stays server-authoritative while gold features reuse the client save.
