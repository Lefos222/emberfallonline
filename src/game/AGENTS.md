- Farm plots are drawn over the painted field in `src/assets/farm-estate.jpg`; `PLOT_SPOTS` in `src/game/Farm.tsx` holds each plot centre as a % of the picture, so replacing the art means re-measuring them. Seed kinds and their gold/Farm XP payouts all live in `src/game/farm.ts` (`SEED_KINDS`, `seedGold`, `seedXp`). Level perks (`FARM_PERKS`), rare harvests and Hearth feasts live there too; feasts reach combat via `feastFx` beside `potionFx` in `computeStats`.
- Mounts (`src/game/mounts.ts`), runes (`runes.ts`), the VIP Black Market (`black-market.ts`) and the Wheel of Fortune share one panel module, `src/game/Emporium.tsx`. Gold mounts live in `hero.mounts`, Diamond mounts in server-owned `srv.mounts` via `premium_service('mount_<id>')` whose SQL price table mirrors `MOUNTS`; the wheel prize is rolled only by the `wheel_spin` RPC; rune ranks join Renown's slot in `computeStats`. Why: Diamond spending stays server-authoritative while gold features reuse the client save.
- Materials occupy bag slots (one per `MAT_STACK` per type, `matSlots`/`invCap` in `src/game/data.ts`); the shared Guild Vault (3 pages x 256, table `guild_vault`) moves gear only through `guild_vault_deposit`/`guild_vault_withdraw` RPCs, whose bag-room check (`_bag_room` SQL) mirrors `invCap`; gems and chests also take one slot per kind, and material/gem/chest stacks deposit via `guild_vault_deposit_stack`. Why: vault items must not be duplicable from the client.
- Player castle raids are settled by the `player_castle_raid` RPC (3 a day, 8h per target, plunder minted by the server, defenders lose nothing) and logged in `castle_raids`; wall level (`hero.worldCastle.fort`) is client-paid gold and only scales the defender and the plunder. Why: raids must not be forgeable or drain other players.
- Guild boss raids, weekly guild quests and the weekly guild tournament are server-owned (`drizzle/migrations/0030_guild_raids_quests_tournament.sql`, client `src/game/guild-events.ts`, panel `GuildEvents.tsx` as the Guild Hall's `events` room); clients report only win/loss and every reward is minted by a claim RPC. Why: shared guild rewards must not be forgeable.
- Epic Armours (`src/game/epic-armour.ts`, tab in the Forge beside Synthesis) are ordinary `Item`s tagged with `Item.epic`; `itemStats` multiplies by the set's `mult`, so no second stat engine exists. Their materials each drop from exactly one source.
- The Party Spire (`src/game/spire-party.ts`, panel `SpireGate.tsx` as the War Gates' fourth tab, SQL `drizzle/migrations/0032_spire_party.sql` + `0033_spire_party_fixes.sql`) is server-owned: `spire_begin` spends one of three runs per UTC day, `spire_clear` pays a Spire Chest only for the next floor in order inside a four-hour run (at most 240), and `spire_open_chest` mints a reward scaled by `spire_runs.best`. The fight itself is one continuous 1-4 hero battle driven by the party leader's `tick()` and passed to the others over the Realtime channel in `spire-sync.ts`, so every hero spends their own run and reports their own clears; a closed channel simply leaves a party of one. Each hero fights with their own `buildKit` abilities, mapped onto the live fight by `spireKit` (turn cooldowns become milliseconds, self buffs become a short guard), so the Spire never grows a second skill list; the panel counts a chest the moment the server confirms its floor, and the leader re-broadcasts the roster and answers a repeated greeting with the live fight snapshot, so a message dropped anywhere in the climb heals itself; only the leader's `bye` disbands the party, because another hero leaving must never tear down everyone else's fight. Why: shared party rewards must not be forgeable, and a client's `tick` never pays anything by itself.
- Guild bank, 24-hour guild buffs, contribution shop, daily guild dice and contribution ranks are server-owned (`drizzle/migrations/0031_*`, client `src/game/guild-extras.ts`, panel `GuildExtras.tsx` as the Guild Events "bank" tab); SQL `_buff_cost`/`_guild_shop`/`_guild_rank` mirror `GUILD_BUFFS`/`GUILD_SHOP`/`GUILD_RANKS`, and buffs reach combat via `guildBuffFx` beside `feastFx`. Why: shared guild rewards must not be forgeable.
- The Wheel of Fortune prize table lives in `src/game/wheel.ts`; the server `wheel_spin` (latest: `drizzle/migrations/0035_wheel_classic_rng.sql`) must use the same segment numbers and weights, and the slice order of `src/assets/wheel-of-fortune-16.webp` follows that table (segment `i` at `i × 22.5°` clockwise from the pointer). `src/test/wheel-luck.test.ts` checks the server odds against the table.

- Arena Clash (`src/game/arena-event.ts`, panel `ArenaEvent.tsx`, SQL `drizzle/migrations/0045_arena_party_event.sql`) opens for one hour at 00/06/12/18 UTC; parties of 1-4 form by code, the leader queues, and `arena_event_queue` pairs same-size parties and picks the winner server-side; every client replays the same seeded fight on the server clock, and only `arena_event_claim` pays gold (loser 40%), keep `arenaEventGold` equal to the SQL formula. Why: PvP rewards must not be forgeable.
- The Minigame Hall (`src/game/Minigames.tsx`, limits in `minigames.ts`) holds every non-battle pastime, including the Fishing Pier; the farm no longer links to fishing. Why: one place for pastimes.
- A party climb never ends because one hero fell: `tick()` in `spire-party.ts` only sets `outcome: "fallen"` when every hero is down, a fallen hero is skipped by foes and by their own casts, and the floor-clear breather revives them at a quarter health (guards and cooldowns cleared). The panel says so when the local hero is down, so nobody leaves a party that is still climbing.
- Every trained track is worth 1% more than its own card: `UPGRADE_BR_BOOST` in `data.ts` scales the Guild Hall training (`guildFx`), Barracks drills and stat trainings, attribute drills, Magic Tower Astrals (`astralFx`) and Runes (`runeFx`) inside `computeStats`. Raise it together with the anticheat Battle Rating caps (`src/game/anticheat-limits.ts` and `0043_anticheat.sql`) when it grows.
- Levels past `LATE_CURVE_LEVEL` 40 cost an extra `LATE_CURVE_PER_LEVEL` (0.15%) of their fight count per level (`fightsToLevel`), about a seventh more fights by the cap — a steeper late climb, never a wall.
- The guide is data: chapters and steps live in `guide.ts`, their pictures and step glyphs in `guide-art.tsx` (a new chapter needs both), and `searchGuide` reads the same tables, so the search box can never drift from the text.
- Party invites for the Party Spire and Arena Clash (`src/game/party-invites.ts`, UI `PartyInvite.tsx`, popup mounted once in `Game.tsx`) travel over a per-hero Realtime broadcast channel `party-invites-<heroId>`, are shown only when the sender is on the friend list, and Accept only hands the code to the panel, which joins through its normal path (Spire channel / `arena_event_join`); a chat message with the code goes along as the offline fallback. Why: invites need no table and a forged invite can never grant anything.
