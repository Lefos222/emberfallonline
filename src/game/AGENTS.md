- Farm plots are drawn over the painted field in `src/assets/farm-estate.jpg`; `PLOT_SPOTS` in `src/game/Farm.tsx` holds each plot centre as a % of the picture, so replacing the art means re-measuring them. Seed kinds and their gold/Farm XP payouts all live in `src/game/farm.ts` (`SEED_KINDS`, `seedGold`, `seedXp`). Level perks (`FARM_PERKS`), rare harvests and Hearth feasts live there too; feasts reach combat via `feastFx` beside `potionFx` in `computeStats`.
- Mounts (`src/game/mounts.ts`), runes (`runes.ts`), the VIP Black Market (`black-market.ts`) and the Wheel of Fortune share one panel module, `src/game/Emporium.tsx`. Gold mounts live in `hero.mounts`, Diamond mounts in server-owned `srv.mounts` via `premium_service('mount_<id>')` whose SQL price table mirrors `MOUNTS`; the wheel prize is rolled only by the `wheel_spin` RPC; rune ranks join Renown's slot in `computeStats`. Why: Diamond spending stays server-authoritative while gold features reuse the client save.
- Materials occupy bag slots (one per `MAT_STACK` per type, `matSlots`/`invCap` in `src/game/data.ts`); the shared Guild Vault (3 pages x 256, table `guild_vault`) moves gear only through `guild_vault_deposit`/`guild_vault_withdraw` RPCs, whose bag-room check (`_bag_room` SQL) mirrors `invCap`; gems and chests also take one slot per kind, and material/gem/chest stacks deposit via `guild_vault_deposit_stack`. Why: vault items must not be duplicable from the client.
- Player castle raids are settled by the `player_castle_raid` RPC (3 a day, 8h per target, plunder minted by the server, defenders lose nothing) and logged in `castle_raids`; wall level (`hero.worldCastle.fort`) is client-paid gold and only scales the defender and the plunder. Why: raids must not be forgeable or drain other players.
- Guild boss raids, weekly guild quests and the weekly guild tournament are server-owned (`drizzle/migrations/0030_guild_raids_quests_tournament.sql`, client `src/game/guild-events.ts`, panel `GuildEvents.tsx` as the Guild Hall's `events` room); clients report only win/loss and every reward is minted by a claim RPC. Why: shared guild rewards must not be forgeable.
- Epic Armours (`src/game/epic-armour.ts`, tab in the Forge beside Synthesis) are ordinary `Item`s tagged with `Item.epic`; `itemStats` multiplies by the set's `mult`, so no second stat engine exists. Their materials each drop from exactly one source.
